{#- Overrides Material's partials/footer.html. Material omits the previous/next link entirely at the ends of the nav, which makes the footer jump around: the first page shows one control on the right, the last shows one on the left. This renders both positions always, and marks the unavailable direction as disabled — so the controls stay put and a reader can see there is nothing further rather than wondering where the button went. A disabled control is a , not an : it has no href to follow, is not in the tab order, and carries aria-disabled for assistive technology. -#}
